Simple AH
=========

Auction house prices for WoW Forever (EU), refreshed every hour.

Install
  1. Copy the SimpleAH folder into
       World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns
     (usually C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns)
  2. Double-click SimpleAH.exe.
     Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app is not signed:
     click "More info", then "Run anyway".
     It downloads prices, starts with Windows and keeps them fresh in the background.

In game
  /reload          load the newest prices (or just log in)
  /ah              open the search (or click the key button in the window to bind a key)
  Shift-click      any item while the search is open to look it up
  /ah market       choose another ruleset or the Blackwater auction house
  /ah help         all commands

Prices come from the official Blizzard auction API. Your character picks the
Alliance or Horde auction house automatically.

Uninstall
  Run Uninstall.bat, then delete Interface\AddOns\SimpleAH.
